# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone who works for Atlas Logistics sp. z o.o., including temporary staff and contractors. It explains how we treat colleagues, customers and partners.

## Respect and fair treatment

We do not tolerate discrimination, harassment or bullying. Concerns can be raised with your manager, the HR department or the Compliance Officer. Raising a concern in good faith never leads to retaliation.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept or give a gift or hospitality only if its value does not exceed PLN 150 per person and it cannot be seen as influencing a business decision. Cash and cash equivalents are never allowed. Anything above PLN 150 must be declined or reported to the Compliance Officer within 5 working days.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when a personal relationship or outside activity could affect your judgment at work. Report any potential conflict to your manager and the Compliance Officer within 14 days of becoming aware of it. Examples include owning more than 5% of a supplier or working for a competitor.

## Speaking up

Suspected violations of law or of this Code can be reported to the Compliance Officer, Helena Bielecka, or anonymously through the speak-up form on the intranet. Reports are acknowledged within 7 days and investigated confidentially.

## Use of company assets

Company laptops, vehicles and accounts are provided for business use. Limited private use of company equipment is allowed if it does not affect work, cost or security. Confidential information never leaves company systems.
